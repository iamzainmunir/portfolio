About Zain Munir

I own the hard technical calls behind products people actually use, and I keep the systems simple enough for a small team to run for years.

I am a backend and distributed systems engineer in Karachi. For six years I have built backend systems, most recently the services behind an EdTech platform used by schools daily, plus payment platforms, ERP integrations, and data work across EdTech and e-commerce.

My work is decomposition: taking a tangled monolith and turning it into independent microservices with clear boundaries, secure by default with JWT and RBAC, observable with proper tracing and logging, and cheap to run. I care about the trade-offs a real team lives with, not the architecture that only looks good on a slide.

I also build with AI every day. I ship AI features into production with the OpenAI API, RAG and embeddings, use AI tooling across the whole workflow, and I am building Shipwright, an autonomous multi-agent system that takes a ticket all the way to a shipped app.

Flagship / in production

SimpliEd, a school platform for 25k+ people

Software Architect and Senior Backend, Jan 2023 to present

Problem

A tightly coupled platform ran attendance, fees, invoicing and an LMS for real schools. It was risky to change and hard to scale.

Approach

I decomposed it into NestJS microservices behind a secure middleware layer, added a universal payment service any partner bank can plug into, a hardened webhook receiver, and a 40+ processor job service for PDFs, push, SMS and attendance.

Impact

Daily operations for 25,000+ students, parents and teachers. A full migration off the legacy system with zero data loss, and 100+ endpoints documented for mobile and partner teams.
